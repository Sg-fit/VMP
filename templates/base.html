Before you start

A few things worth knowing

Think of this as a quick second pair of ears. It's good at sparking ideas, but it isn't a transcription service. Here's what to expect:

  • It's built for instrumental music. It doesn't pick out lyrics, and if someone's singing, the melody it finds will usually be their voice.
  • We write out the drums and the lowest part: the bass, or the low notes of a solo instrument. Everything else shows up in the chords and the melody line rather than as its own sheet music.
  • Phone recordings lose a lot of low end, so a bass guitar and a guitar's low strings can sound the same. Tick the instruments you hear when you upload and we'll go with what you tell us.
  • Treat the numbers as good guesses. A key can get mixed up with its relative (C major and A minor share their notes), tempo sometimes comes out at half or double speed, chords get simplified, and section letters are a rough map. For slow, smooth music with few clear attacks (strings, pads, soft piano) we mark the tempo as approximate.
  • We recognise 8-, 12- and 16-bar blues (major and minor) and the 32-bar AABA song form. Anything else is shown as sections: A, B, C. Odd time signatures like 5/4 and 7/4 need a clear beat, such as drums, before we'll call them.
  • If the melody matters, type it in. Picking a tune out of a full band automatically is hit and miss.
  • AI ideas are optional, and sometimes slow or unavailable. We check any times and chords they mention against your track. Their opinions are their own.
  • Find music like this: we listen to the songs you upload. For songs from a playlist link or a list we only read the names and look up the tags other listeners gave them, usually for the artist rather than the song, so they count for less. Very obscure songs might not turn up at all.
  • We delete your audio as soon as we're done with it. Results stay for a day, or for as long as you like if you save them to a free account.
