Before you start

A few things worth knowing

Think of it as a quick second pair of ears: great for ideas, not a transcription service.

  • Made for instrumental music. It shows when someone sings, but not the words, and the tune it finds will usually be the singer's.
  • We write out the drums and the lowest part (the bass, or a solo instrument's low notes). Everyone else shows up in the chords and the tune.
  • Phones lose the low end, so a bass and a guitar's low strings can sound alike. Tick what you hear when you upload and we'll go with you.
  • The numbers are good guesses. A key can swap with its relative (C major, A minor), tempo can come out at half or double, and chords get simplified.
  • If the tune matters, type it in. Picking it out of a full band is hit and miss.
  • AI ideas are optional and sometimes slow. We check any times and chords they mention.
  • Your audio is deleted as soon as we're done. Results stay a day, or as long as you like in a free account.
