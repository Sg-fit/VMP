      How we come up with the tags
      • Songs you upload, we listen to: tempo, time signature, mood and energy, drums or not, blues forms and, where the style model is installed, styles, moods and instruments. These count in full.
      • Songs from a link or a list, we look up by name on MusicBrainz. Those tags usually describe the artist rather than the song, so each counts 0.4 against 1. Upload the audio too and we'll listen to it.
      • Limits: up to 100 songs; no private playlists; very new or obscure songs may not be on MusicBrainz yet. Broad tags like “rock” are shown but not searched: they lead straight back to the biggest names.
      • Style model: Essentia by MTG, Universitat Pompeu Fabra — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial use.

      What ticking a box changes
      • Drums and bass decide whether you get a drum grid and a bass line.
      • One instrument on its own (solo guitar, piano, cello): its lowest notes get written out as their own part, in guitar notation for a guitar, with that instrument's sound in the MIDI file.
      • Voice, or one lead instrument: we label the melody line as theirs.
      • What it can't do: apart from the drums and the lowest part, instruments aren't written out one by one. A guitar or piano in a band shows up in the chords and the melody.
