Privacy

Your unreleased music stays yours.

Most of what people bring here hasn't been released. So the app is built private by default: your audio is gone the moment it's analysed, your results are yours alone, and nothing that could identify your songs leaves our server.

{{ icon('trash', 22) }}Audio deleted at onceYour recording, and any song you compare it with, is deleted as soon as it has been analysed. We never keep, play or share it.
{{ icon('lock', 22) }}Results only you can openA result opens only in the browser that made it, or in your account once saved. Sharing by link is a switch you turn on.
{{ icon('shield', 22) }}No names leave the serverThe optional AI ideas get the analysis numbers (key, chords, tempo, sections), never your audio, file names or titles.
{{ icon('ban', 22) }}No ads, no trackersNo analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts, nothing sold or shared. Your music is never used to train anything.

{{ icon('server', 24) }}What leaves our server

  • {{ icon('spark', 22) }}
    AI ideas ({{ 'switched on here' if ai_on else 'switched off on this server' }}; you can untick them for any upload): the analysis numbers go to the AI provider to write suggestions. No audio, no file names, no song titles, no account details.
  • {{ icon('compass', 22) }}
    “Find music like this”, only when you use it: the song names and playlist links you type are looked up on MusicBrainz, ListenBrainz and Last.fm, and a public Spotify or YouTube playlist is read for its song names. Songs you upload there are analysed here, like everything else.
  • {{ icon('server', 22) }}
    Nothing else. The style and instrument models run on our own server. Pages load nothing from other sites.

{{ icon('shield', 24) }}How the site protects it

  • {{ icon('eyeoff', 22) }}
    Result pages, your library and your profile tell search engines not to index them and browsers not to keep copies in shared caches.
  • {{ icon('link', 22) }}
    When you follow a link out of a result (a search link, say), the other site isn't told where you came from, so a result's address can't leak.
  • {{ icon('lock', 22) }}
    Two small cookies only: your sign-in (it also holds this browser's private key to its own results), and whether you've closed the welcome note.
  • {{ icon('chart', 22) }}
    Like any website, the server's log records which pages were requested and from where. The site runs behind Cloudflare, which carries the traffic.
