Your memory

What we remember

With your memory on, the app learns from your saved analyses, your corrections and the ideas you rate, and uses that on your next uploads. It's all listed here: change or delete anything, or switch it off.

How we use it

  • Upload form: your usual instruments and genre are ticked for you. You can change them each time.
  • Next to your earlier tracks: each new track is compared with your own saved ones: a first minor key, a faster tempo than ever, chords you haven't used before.
  • Close calls only: when a measurement is unsure (say, the beat was hard to find), we tell you what your history suggests. We never change what the audio clearly shows.
  • Ideas: ones you marked 👎 are dropped, kinds you liked come first, and ones you've seen before go last.
  • AI ideas: the AI gets a short summary of your habits, your goals and what helped you, so it can push you somewhere new. It never sees your audio.
